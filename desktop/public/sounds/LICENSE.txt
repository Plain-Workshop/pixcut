Bundled recorded sound effects.

Only files whose licence allows commercial use AND redistribution are accepted:
  CC0 1.0     public domain dedication, no credit needed
  CC BY 4.0   free for commercial videos, the author must be credited

Every file is listed in manifest.json with its author, original page and licence.
PixCut shows the credit line for CC BY sounds and adds it to the export credits.
Files are trimmed, loudness-normalised and re-encoded to mono AAC; the source
recordings are otherwise unchanged. Add sounds with scripts/import-sounds.mjs.

Sites whose licence forbids redistributing the raw file (Pixabay, Mixkit,
Zapsplat, Sonniss) and any NC or ND licence are not used.

Sources in this folder
