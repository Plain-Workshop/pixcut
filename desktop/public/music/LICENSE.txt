Bundled music — CC BY 4.0 (attribution required)

These are real recordings by independent artists, not public-domain works. They
are free to use in personal and commercial videos, but the artist must be
credited wherever the video is published — usually the video description.
PixCut copies the exact credit line from the Music panel and the export studio.

Each file is a 110-second excerpt of the artist's published track, loudness
normalised to -16 LUFS, re-encoded as AAC 128 kbps. The edits and the encoding
are changes to the original; the artist's copyright and the licence are
unchanged.
